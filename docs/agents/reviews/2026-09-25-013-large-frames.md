# Large frames

## Scope and coverage

Commit `b6e8d5c`, "Decode frames of any length": `crates/ur-client/src/frame.rs`, its tests, the
plan `docs/agents/plans/2026-09-25-012-large-frames.md`, and the work log. I also read the codec's
two users, the `Client` reader in `crates/ur-client/src/client.rs` and each socket connection's
reader and writer in `crates/ur/src/daemon/server.rs`, and the wire protocol section of
`docs/agents/architecture.md` and the Frame entry in `docs/agents/glossary.md`.

Lenses: correctness, error-handling, testing, documentation, and simplicity.

## Findings

No findings. The decoder now accepts every length its encoder writes, and the unknown tag and short
`PTY` frame checks remain. Neither codec user adds its own size limit, so a large session snapshot
or prompt now reaches the other end of the socket. The architecture document and the glossary never
stated the 16 MiB limit, so they need no update. `frames_round_trip` covers a 20 MiB `JSON` frame,
which is over the old limit.
